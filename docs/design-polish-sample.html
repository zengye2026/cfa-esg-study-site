教材 Q1 Ch.1 Short-termism

Which of the following investment approaches is most likely to be at risk of short-termism?

以下哪一种投资方法最可能面临短期主义风险？

A.ESG investing
ESG 投资
B.Impact investing
影响力投资
C.Conventional investing
传统投资
回答正确 正确答案：C

解析

C is correct. Conventional financial investing is subject to short-termism, where investors trade based on short-term price movements rather than long-term value.

C 正确。传统金融投资更容易受短期主义影响，投资者更关注短期价格波动，而不是长期价值创造。

英文题眼

risk of short-termism 指向 conventional investing。

中文反推

看到短期主义风险，优先排除 ESG 和 impact investing。

P300 Q35 Ch.2 Asset classes 错题本

Which asset class had the highest allocation in global ESG investing in 2018?

2018 年全球 ESG 投资中，哪类资产配置占比最高？

A.Real estate
房地产
B.Public equity
上市股票
C.Private equity / venture capital
私募股权 / 风险投资
回答错误 正确答案：B

解析

Listed equity has the highest allocation of asset classes, followed by fixed income. Real estate has a much lower allocation.

上市股票在 ESG 投资资产类别中占比最高，其次是固定收益。房地产占比较低。

中文反推

问资产类别最高占比，优先想到上市股票，不是房地产。

易错提醒

Real estate 和 Private equity / VC 占比都低，容易被题目中的熟悉名词带偏。
