Policy Choices：目标

金融监管是政策选择的下游。

政策制定者回应 sustainability risks and opportunities。

Financial Regulation：规则

监管关注市场秩序、投资者保护、金融部门有序扩张。

四大主题：corporate disclosure、stewardship、asset owners、investor protection。

Financial Supervision：监督

金融监督是金融监管的下游。

看银行、保险、基金、养老金是否按气候和 ESG 要求管理风险、披露过程和结果。

Global Tools：工具箱

TCFD、IFRS S1/S2、CSRD、EU Taxonomy、SFDR、SDR、stewardship codes、stress tests。

Market Actors：执行者

Investees 管理 ESG 风险并决定披露；governments 引导资本；civil society 施压；academia 验证商业逻辑。

Policy Trend：趋势

从 voluntary 到 mandatory，从 comply or explain 到 comply and explain，从 policy 到 implementation/reporting。

1. Risks

气候、生物多样性、资源稀缺影响金融稳定和单个组合。

2. Policy

政府和国际目标设定方向，例如 Paris Agreement、SDGs。

3. Regulation

披露、分类法、投资者保护、stewardship 进入规则。

4. Supervision

监管机构用压力测试、报告要求、资本和风险管理要求推动执行。

5. Market

投资者更能识别可持续资产，市场更能定价 ESG 风险。

Fiduciary Cases & Other Stakeholders 受托责任案例和其他主体

✓
监管者若狭义解释 fiduciary duty，可能对 ESG investing 形成挑战。
✓
US ERISA 对 private sector retirement plans 的 fiduciaries 设定 prudent-person standard。
✓
ERISA fiduciaries 应 solely in the interest of participants/beneficiaries 行事。
✓
ERISA fiduciaries 应以 prudent person care、skill、diligence 投资，并 diversify to minimize large losses。
✓
DOL 1994 IB：ETIs 若预期回报与同风险替代投资相当，可与 ERISA fiduciary obligations 相容，即 all things being equal test。
✓
DOL 2008：collateral non-economic factors 应少见，并需文件证明符合 ERISA rigorous fiduciary standards。
✓
DOL 2015 IB 确认 ETIs 可基于 collateral benefits 投资，只要投资适合计划且经济/财务上等同；ESG 有时直接关系经济财务价值。
✓
DOL 2020 final rule 删除 ESG 术语，使用 non-financial objectives，并认为 ESG 缺乏统一定义。
✓
DOL 2020 强调 tie-breaker extremely rare；ESG only if material economic risks/opportunities；并增加 documentation requirements。
✓
DOL 2021 non-enforcement；2021 new rule 允许 private sector retirement plans 考虑 ESG criteria and proxy voting。
✓
DOL 2022 final rule clarifies fiduciaries may consider climate/ESG when relevant to risk-return analysis。
✓
UK Kay Review 2012 强调 long-term decision making、trust、stewardship，并指出 fiduciary duty 被误解和误用的损害。
✓
UK Law Commission 2014：trustees may and should consider financially material ESG factors。
✓
UK non-financial factors 只有在 members likely share concern 且 no significant financial detriment 时才可考虑。
✓
UK Stewardship Code 2020 适用于 asset owners、asset managers、service providers，并要求系统整合 stewardship、investment、material ESG issues、climate change。
✓
EU Shareholder Rights Directive II 2019 旨在提高投资者与 investee companies 的 engagement 水平和质量。
✓
SRD II 要求 engagement policy，并每年报告 strategy integration、dialogue、voting/shareholder rights、collaboration、conflicts of interest。
✓
Investees 包括 companies、projects、agencies、jurisdictions。
✓
Investees 决策者影响 ESG risks 管理、对环境和社会的 impact，以及 ESG disclosure level。
✓
ESG investing 最紧迫问题之一是缺乏 reliable and consistent ESG data。
✓
Governments 认为 RI 通过社保/公共养老金压力、公共基础设施需求、低碳转型资本迁移三方面支持社会结果。
✓
Civil society/NGOs 推动公司可持续性，提高对投资社会和环境影响的透明度要求。
✓
Civil society 有的与投资公司和监管者合作，有的揭示不足以应对全球挑战的行动。
✓
Academia 验证 ESG integration 的 business case，并研究 ESG 对投资回报和金融市场的影响。
Regulatory Framework

监管框架：四大主题 + 工具箱

这部分先背结构，不急着背国家。考试喜欢把主题、目标和例子混在一起问。
1. 政策、监管、监督的上下游
downstream

原文要点：Financial regulation is downstream from policy choices；financial supervision is downstream from financial regulation。

中文解释：政策先回答“社会想把金融引向哪里”；监管再写成规则；监督则检查金融机构是否真的执行。

Policy chain
==
policy choices → financial regulation → financial supervision → market conduct
Regulator focus
==
orderly markets + investor protection + orderly sector expansion
考点提醒：这是一句总纲。遇到问政策和监管关系，先想 downstream chain。
2. 四大监管主题
four themes

原文要点：Sustainability-related financial regulations relevant to investment generally involve corporate disclosure、stewardship、asset owners、investor protection。

中文解释：监管不是只管“披露”。它还管投资者如何与公司互动、资产所有者如何管理 ESG、投资者是否被充分保护。

Financial regulation
==
corporate disclosure + stewardship + asset owners + investor protection
Investor protection
==
transparency + market efficiency + sustainability preference/product clarity
题眼钩子：“protect investors by improving transparency and efficiency” 对应 investor protection。
3. Corporate disclosure
material ESG risks

原文要点：Corporate disclosure guidelines 通常来自 governments 或 stock exchanges，要求公司披露 material ESG risks。

中文解释：披露的目的不是“写报告好看”，而是让投资者能把 ESG 风险放进投资决策。

Disclosure
==
material ESG risk information → better investment decisions
Examples
==
TCFD + GRI + IFRS S1/S2 + NFRD/CSRD
投资影响：披露标准越统一，投资者越容易比较公司，ESG data gap 越小。
4. Stewardship regulation
interaction rules

原文要点：Stewardship regulation governs interactions between investors and investee companies。

中文解释：它管的是投资者持股后怎么和公司互动，例如 engagement、voting、dialogue、conflicts。

Stewardship
==
investor-investee interaction + shareholder/beneficiary protection + market stability
Trend
==
mostly voluntary codes → mandatory regulation in Europe
5. Asset owners regulation
pensions / insurers

原文要点：Asset owner regulation 通常聚焦 pension funds 和 insurers，要求整合 ESG factors 并披露 process and outcome。

中文解释：养老金和保险公司资产规模大、负债期限长，气候风险和 ESG 风险会直接影响长期偿付能力和受益人利益。

AO regulation
==
pension funds + insurers + ESG integration + process/outcome disclosure
Climate concern
==
insurance market + wider financial industry risk
6. Exhibit 10 的监管工具箱
toolkit

原文要点：监管工具把 high-level sustainability policy targets 与金融监管干预连接起来。

中文解释：政策目标像目的地，工具箱像交通工具：披露标准、taxonomies、压力测试、资本要求、风险管理、税费和补贴都可以把资金引向可持续活动。

Toolkit
==
disclosure/accounting standards + taxonomies + risk management/stress tests/capital + taxes/subsidies + legal investment rules
Purpose
==
awareness + asset identification + better pricing of sustainability risks

Fiduciary Duty

受托责任：ESG 能不能纳入，关键看是否与风险回报相关

PDF 用美国和英国案例说明：狭义解释 fiduciary duty 会阻碍 ESG；更成熟的解释强调 financially material ESG factors 应被考虑。
1. 为什么受托责任重要
permission boundary

原文要点：Challenges to ESG investing can emerge if regulators hold a narrow interpretation of fiduciary duty。

中文解释：如果监管者把 ESG 简单看成“非财务偏好”，受托人就会担心纳入 ESG 是违背受益人财务利益。

Fiduciary tension
==
ESG as financial risk/opportunity vs ESG as non-financial objective
Modern direction
==
financially material ESG factors should be considered
2. US ERISA 与 DOL 摆动
pendulum

原文要点：ERISA 要求 fiduciaries 仅为参与者和受益人利益行事、谨慎投资、分散投资。DOL 对 ESG 的立场从 ETI 兼容，到 2020 年收紧，再到 2022 年明确可在 risk-return relevant 时考虑 ESG。

中文解释：美国案例像钟摆：ESG 可以被接受，但前提是要讲得清楚它和风险回报有关。

ERISA duty
==
sole interest + prudent care/skill/diligence + diversification
DOL line
==
1994 ETI allowed → 2020 non-financial caution → 2022 ESG allowed if risk-return relevant
易错：2020 规则不是说所有 ESG 都不能考虑，而是 ESG 只有在 material economic risks/opportunities 时才能作为 financial factor。
3. UK：财务重大 ESG 应被考虑
financial materiality

原文要点：Law Commission 2014 认为 trustees may take into account financially material ESG factors；并进一步认为 should take into account financially material factors。

中文解释：英国逻辑更明确：如果 ESG 是财务重大因素，不是“可以考虑一下”，而是履责时应当考虑。

UK fiduciary logic
==
financially material ESG → trustees should consider
Non-financial factors
==
members share concern + no significant financial detriment
4. UK Stewardship Code 与 EU SRD II
engagement reporting

原文要点：UK Stewardship Code 2020 要求系统整合 stewardship and investment，包括 material ESG issues and climate change。EU Shareholder Rights Directive II 要求 engagement policy 和年度报告。

中文解释：受托责任不只是“买什么”，也包括“持有后怎么负责地互动”。

Stewardship Code
==
asset owners + asset managers + service providers + ESG/climate integration
SRD II report
==
strategy + dialogue + voting/rights + collaboration + conflicts
Other Stakeholders

其他主体：被投公司给数据，政府引资本，社会和学术界给压力与证据

最后一组主体不是金融中介，却决定 ESG 市场是否有真实信息、真实项目、社会监督和研究基础。
1. Investees
data source

原文要点：Investees include companies、projects、agencies、jurisdictions。

中文解释：投资者想分析 ESG，第一手数据很多来自被投对象。被投公司和项目既管理 ESG 风险，也决定披露多少信息。

Investees
==
companies + projects + agencies + jurisdictions
Investees role
==
manage ESG risks/impact + decide disclosure level
考点提醒：ESG investing 的紧迫问题之一是 reliable and consistent ESG data 不足。
2. Governments
capital direction

原文要点：Governments 看到 responsible investment 在社保/养老金、公共基础设施、低碳转型三方面支持社会结果。

中文解释：政府需要长期资本帮助解决养老金缺口、基础设施建设和低碳转型，因此会鼓励投资行业考虑 ESG 的财务重大性和社会环境影响。

Government need
==
pension stability + infrastructure funding + low-carbon transition capital
Policy route
==
encourage ESG materiality + social/environmental impact + national priorities
3. Civil Society
pressure + partnership

原文要点：Civil society including NGOs 推动公司层面 sustainability，也要求更透明地考虑 investment 对社会和环境的影响。

中文解释：NGO 一方面可以与投资机构和监管者合作，帮助理解具体 ESG 问题；另一方面也会曝光不足以应对全球挑战的行动。

Civil society
==
company sustainability pressure + investment impact transparency demand + expert partnership + public challenge
4. Academia
evidence base

原文要点：Academic research has been influential in validating the business case for integrating ESG factors into the investment process。

中文解释：学术界提供证据：ESG 因素如何进入投资决策、如何影响投资回报、如何影响更广金融市场。

Academia
==
business case validation + ESG-return studies + market impact research
Traps & Self-Test

易错点与自测题

优先使用 PDF Practice Problems 对应题眼：Q3、Q21、Q22、Q27、Q28、Q29。
易错 1：TCFD 四块没有 credit ratingsTCFD = governance、strategy、risk management、metrics and targets。
易错 2：EU Taxonomy 不是贡献三个目标它要求 substantial contribution to at least one objective，并且 DNSH 其他目标。
易错 3：Investor protection 的目标不是限制信息，而是通过 transparency and efficiency 保护投资者。
易错 4：CGT 的目标不是减少欧中碳足迹本身，而是建立绿色经济活动的共同理解，便利资本流向 sustainable solutions。
易错 5：Comply or explain它不是比 comply and explain 更严格，反而给了挑战 ESG integration required 的空间。
易错 6：受托责任ESG 若是 financially material，就不是“非财务爱好”，而是可能应纳入风险回报分析。
Q3
Which established a framework that lays out the conditions for economic activities to be considered environmentally sustainable?A. EU Taxonomy Regulation. B. Climate Disclosure Standards Board. C. TCFD.
答案：A。EU Taxonomy Regulation 建立了判断经济活动是否 environmentally sustainable 的框架。逻辑等式：EU Taxonomy == green activity conditions + six environmental objectives。
Q21
The “comply or explain” approach to ESG investment policies:A. requires mandatory disclosures. B. allows challenges to the assertion that ESG integration is required. C. is more stringent than comply and explain.
答案：B。Comply or explain 方式给投资者留下空间，继续挑战“ESG integration 是必需”的说法。逻辑等式：comply or explain == comply or justify non-compliance → more challenge room。
Q22
Which is one EU Taxonomy condition for an economic activity to be environmentally sustainable?A. Complying with highest social/governance standards. B. Doing no significant harm to any environmental objective. C. Contributing substantially to at least three environmental objectives.
答案：B。三条件是：substantial contribution to at least one of six objectives；doing no significant harm to the others；complying with minimum EU-specified social and governance safeguards。题眼钩子：DNSH = EU Taxonomy。
Q27
What is the aim of investor protection regulations that consider sustainability?A. Protect investors by improving transparency and efficiency. B. Restrict sustainability information. C. Prevent investors from incorporating sustainability preferences.
答案：A。Investor protection regulations aim to protect investors by improving transparency and efficiency in markets。逻辑等式：investor protection == transparency + efficiency + preference/product clarity。
Q28
Which is not a key area of focus in the TCFD recommendations?A. Governance. B. Risk management. C. Credit ratings.
答案：C。TCFD 四块是 Governance、Strategy、Risk management、Metrics and targets。Credit ratings 属于评级/金融服务机构相关内容，不是 TCFD 的四大领域。
Q29
The Common Ground Taxonomy established by the EU and China aims to:A. Increase transparency for investors. B. Establish a shared understanding of what constitutes a green economic activity. C. Reduce the carbon footprint of both regions.
答案：B。CGT 旨在建立对绿色经济活动的共同理解，帮助引导投资流向可持续解决方案，并降低跨境绿色资本流动中的交易和验证成本。
自测
如果题干说 “financial regulation is downstream from policy choices”，你能反推完整链条吗？
答案：policy choices → financial regulation → financial supervision → disclosure/stewardship/asset-owner rules/investor protection → market behavior。
自测
EU Taxonomy 六大环境目标怎么压缩记？
答案：气候减缓、气候适应、水和海洋、循环经济、污染防控、生物多样性和生态系统。逻辑等式：EU six objectives == climate x2 + water + circular + pollution + biodiversity。
自测
Investees、governments、civil society、academia 分别给 ESG 市场提供什么？
答案：Investees 给 ESG 风险管理和披露数据；governments 给政策和资本方向；civil society 给压力、合作和监督；academia 给研究证据和 business case。
