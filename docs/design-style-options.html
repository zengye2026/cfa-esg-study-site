What is the feature of impact investing?

影响力投资的特征是什么？

A.Focus on generating social and environmental impact only只关注产生社会和环境影响
B.Focus on generating financial returns
C.Both两者都是
回答正确正确答案：C

教材 Q3Ch.1Financial Materiality

ESG integration can have a material financial impact on a company leading to:

A.increased exposure to fines and litigation.
B.reduced risk of fines and litigation.降低罚款和诉讼风险。
C.lower stakeholder engagement.
回答正确正确答案：B你的选择：B

解析

Effective ESG integration can reduce material financial risks, including fines, litigation, and regulatory exposure.

有效的 ESG 整合可以降低重大财务风险，包括罚款、诉讼和监管风险。

逻辑等式

ESG integration → lower material risk → financial impact
